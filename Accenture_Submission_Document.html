1. Project Overview & Introduction

PatientTriage.ai is an emergency department clinical decision-support system and continuous physiological safety surveillance layer. While traditional emergency triage treats patient prioritization as a static, point-in-time snapshot at the hospital front door, PatientTriage.ai continuously monitors patient waiting times, vital sign trajectory velocity (ΔSpO₂, ΔHR), missing information uncertainty, and clinical coverage to answer:

Core Clinical Question: “Who in the waiting room is no longer safe to keep waiting?”

2. The Core Problem: 3 Failure Modes of Traditional Triage

Emergency departments worldwide utilize 5-level triage systems (ESI, Manchester, CTAS) to sort patients on arrival. Once triaged, patients routinely wait 2 to 6 hours before physician evaluation. Traditional static triage fails in three concrete ways:

  • 1. Silent Post-Triage Waiting Room Deterioration: A patient triaged as Level 3 (Urgent) or Level 4 (Less Urgent) may develop acute hypoxemia or occult shock while waiting. Static systems have no automated mechanism to detect or elevate deteriorating patients without manual nurse re-checks.
  • 2. Missing Vitals & Stale Data Assumed Safe: When triage data is missing (e.g. absent pulse oximetry or blood pressure), standard systems default to low-acuity bands, creating false reassurance. Under the clinical reality of “Unknown is NOT Safe”, missing data should heighten vigilance.
  • 3. The Clinical Attention Bottleneck: Static lists rank patients purely by initial acuity. Consequently, critical patients who are already attended by emergency physicians occupy the top slots, while unattended deteriorating patients remain buried.

3. Solution Approach & Innovation

PatientTriage.ai transforms triage into an automated continuous safety surveillance loop:

  1. Rapid Ingestion: Ingests initial vitals, chief complaints, and comorbidity history with age-specific threshold calibration.
  2. Dynamic Staleness Tracking: Observations decay in confidence as time passes (τstaleness), flipping to SAFETY_EXPIRED when safety windows expire.
  3. Delta Deterioration Surveillance: Continuous velocity modeling flags sudden drops in SpO₂ (≤ -5%) or spikes in heart rate.
  4. Attention Gap Live Action Queue: Ranks patients by Need vs. Clinical Coverage, elevating unattended deteriorating cases to Rank #1 with a single next-best-action button.
  5. Clinician Governance & Traceability: Human-in-the-loop override guardrails with mandatory rationale logging to an append-only audit ledger.

5. Core Intelligence Engines & Mathematical Formulations

5.1 Age-Aware Threshold Calibrations

Physiological normal ranges are stratified across three distinct demographic models:

  • Pediatric (<16y): Infant extreme tachycardia (>160 bpm), toddler high fever (≥38.5°C in <3yo alerting for rapid decompensation), and age-adjusted minimum systolic blood pressure: SBP_min = 70 + (2 × Age).
  • Adult (16–64y): Standard clinical shock parameters (SBP < 90, SpO₂ < 92%, RR > 30).
  • Geriatric (≥65y): Blunted autonomic response handling: hypothermia (<36.0°C) alerts for occult sepsis, and shock threshold is adjusted upward (SBP < 100 mmHg).

5.2 Uncertainty-as-Risk Engine (“Unknown ≠ Safe”)

Missing physiological signals actively penalize confidence rather than assuming safety:

Uncertainty = Σ (Missing SpO₂: +22%, Missing SBP: +20%, Missing HR: +15%, Zero History: +18%, Discordance: +14%)
Confidence(t) = max(15.0, 100.0 - Uncertainty)

Asymmetric Safety Bias: If a patient with missing vitals would otherwise fall into Level 4 or 5, the engine escalates them to Level 3 Urgent and mandates an [ ACQUIRE VITALS ] action.

5.3 Dynamic Confidence Decay & Safety Expiry (τstaleness)

Evidence half-life degrades continuously as time passes without fresh observations:

Confidence(t) = Base_Confidence × max(0.20, 1.0 - [ (t - t_last) / (Window × 1.5) ] × 0.65)

Reassessment windows: Level 1 = 5m, Level 2 = 15m, Level 3 = 30m, Level 4 = 60m, Level 5 = 120m. Exceeding the window flips status to EXPIRED (+20 to +35 staleness score).

5.4 The Attention Gap Priority Equation

Action Priority Score = (w_r × Risk + Urgency) + (w_d × Deterioration) + (w_s × Staleness) + Wait Hazard + (w_u × Uncertainty) - (w_c × Clinical Coverage)

Where w_c deducts priority when an attending physician is actively managing the patient (is_attended = True), allowing unattended deteriorating waiting patients to surface to Rank #1.

6. Implementation Details & Tech Stack

  • Backend: Python 3.13.2, FastAPI 0.115+, Pydantic v2 validation, SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and busy timeout resilience.
  • Frontend: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide React icons, Recharts for responsive vital trend sparklines.
  • Design System: Calm clinical light palette (off-white #f8fafc, dark charcoal #0f172a) with high readability and zero eye strain.

11. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does PatientTriage.ai replace the emergency triage nurse?
A: No. It is an assistive continuous safety decision-support layer that augments clinicians by monitoring physiological trajectories and data staleness while patients wait.

Q: How does the system handle patients with zero medical history?
A: Under the “Unknown is NOT Safe” engine, zero medical history incurs an uncertainty penalty, preventing false low-urgency classification and flagging the patient for early physician intake verification.
